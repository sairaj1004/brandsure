# User feedback

Goal: at least 3 real people tried BrandSure on the live site without help. Fill in one block per person, then list what you changed because of it.

## How to run a session (10 minutes each)

1. Send the link https://brandsure.onrender.com/ and the demo password `demo1234`. Say nothing else.
2. Ask them to: (a) log in, (b) paste a short piece of marketing text and run a check, (c) fix or dismiss one finding, (d) submit it for approval.
3. Watch quietly. Write down where they hesitate or ask a question. Do not help unless they are stuck for more than a minute.
4. Ask the questions below.

## Questions

1. In your own words, what does this tool do?
2. What was the first thing you did, and was it obvious what to do next?
3. Did you understand why a finding was raised? (Look at: highlighted text, "Why", "Source".)
4. Was anything confusing or in the wrong place?
5. Did you trust the suggested fixes? Why or why not?
6. What would make you use this at work? What would stop you?
